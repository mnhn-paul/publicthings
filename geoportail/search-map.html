Geoportail search map

Type a place name (e.g. “Wangen”): the geoportail full-text search returns its best proposals as a list, and every one of them is plotted on the geoportail basemap at once. Tick the type filters to hide kinds of results, use “Load more”/“Load all” for additional hits, hover a list entry to highlight its marker, and click one to zoom to it and copy its WGS84 coordinates.

Type at least 2 characters.
