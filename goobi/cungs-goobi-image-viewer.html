Image viewer

Page-by-page viewer for the Josy Cungs field notebooks: zoomable scans with the observation events and taxa recorded on each page.

Options (CORS proxy, record IDs)

A bare record ID such as MNHNL2024-6-01 is expanded to https://face.mnhn.gov.lu/viewer/sourcefile?id=…; the page scans are loaded from the record's IIIF manifest (/viewer/api/v1/records/…/manifest/). Event and taxon data per page come from the METS/MODS file, and taxon names / localities already curated in the METS event viewer are shown here as well.
